PolyTools Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

PolyTools is an Android app by Onova Tech ("we", "us"), called Pocket Tools until November 2026. In short: the tools work entirely on your device, we don't collect any personal data, and the only data that leaves your device on its own is what Google's advertising service uses to show ads.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Your settings (such as theme, language and units), favorites and recently used tools.
  • What you create and save in the tools: notes and lists, reminders, timers and interval sequences, counters, world clock cities, saved GPS places, and the histories some tools keep, such as calculations, QR codes, colors, NFC tags and measurements.
  • Health readings you choose to keep, such as heart rate results, BMI results and step counts. They stay on your device like everything else here.
  • Calibrations of your device's sensors.
  • How many tools you opened today, used only to decide when to show an ad. This count resets every day.
  • Whether you bought "Remove ads" (section 5).

This data is kept only in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. We never receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Device features

Some tools use your device's hardware, such as sensors, the camera and flash (for example, to measure your heart rate from your fingertip), the microphone, NFC or location. Location comes only from your device's GPS. These readings are processed on your device, in real time, and are never sent to us or to anyone else; the camera's images and the microphone's sound are not recorded. A picture you pick from your gallery, for example to read a QR code, is read in memory only and is never stored or sent. When a tool needs a permission, the app asks for it when you use that tool. You can refuse, and the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Files and sharing you start

Data leaves the app only when you ask for it, and only to the place you choose:

  • Export data (Settings → Your data) saves your data to an encrypted file, with an optional password, in a folder you choose. Import data reads such a file back. The app never sends this file anywhere by itself. Ad counters, consent choices and purchases are never put in it.
  • Copy, Share and saving a file (for example a table or a note as text) send exactly what you chose to the clipboard, to the app you pick, or to the folder you pick.
  • Privacy policy in Settings opens this page in your browser.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: at most one full-screen ad for every three tools you open in a day, and never while a tool is in use. To show ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the model, system version and language;
  • your interactions with ads, such as whether an ad was seen or tapped, and information about ad performance.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to personalize them where allowed, and to prevent fraud. It goes to Google, not to us; we only see aggregated reports, such as how many ads were shown. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and read the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Remove ads (purchase through Google Play)

You can buy "Remove ads" once, through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid, and keeps the answer on your device so it also works offline. After the purchase, the app requests no ads and doesn't start Google's advertising service.

6. Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before your data is used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Anywhere, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (on most devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Without an internet connection, every tool keeps working and no ads are requested.

7. Crash and performance statistics

If you allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics about crashes and app performance. We use them only to fix problems. The app itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

8. Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent or share personal data, and we have no servers that receive any. The only third party involved is Google, as described above.

9. Children

PolyTools is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the LGPD in Brazil or the GDPR in Europe), you have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction and deletion. Because we don't hold any of your personal data, everything the app stores can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For data that Google processes for ads, use Google's tools described above. You can also contact us with any question.

11. Changes

We'll update this page when the app changes how it handles data, and change the date at the top.
